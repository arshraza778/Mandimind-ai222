Household Protection: Do's & Don'ts

Monsoon Standard Protocol
check_circle Recommended (करें)
  • bed Sleep under Long-Lasting Insecticide Treated Bed Nets (LLIN) every night.
  • water Boil drinking water for at least 5 minutes, or request free chlorine tablets from Mitanin.
  • medical_services Get tested immediately within 24 hours of fever onset at your village choupal.
cancel Avoid (ना करें)
  • delete_forever Do not let rainwater pool in open tires, discarded earthen pots, or cattle water troughs.
  • medication Never take unverified fever medication without a blood slide or rapid test kit confirmation.
  • do_not_step Do not walk barefoot near swampy fields or defecate near natural drinking springs.

Emergency Fever Guide for Families

Step 1: Do Not Delay Every hour matters with Plasmodium Falciparum. If someone experiences shivering, severe headache, or sudden high fever, contact your Mitanin immediately.
Step 2: Free Complete Treatment If tested positive, complete the 3-day ACT course fully. Do not stop midway even if fever disappears on day 2.
Step 3: Danger Signs for Immediate Hospital Dispatch Loss of consciousness, yellow eyes (jaundice), severe vomiting, or dark blackish urine require 108 ambulance dispatch to Jagdalpur Medical College immediately.

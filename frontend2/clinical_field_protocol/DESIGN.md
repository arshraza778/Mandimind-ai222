## Brand & Style

This design system delivers an operational, mission-critical workspace engineered for district health supervisors, medical officers, and frontline community health worker (Mitanin) networks. The aesthetic balances institutional authority with clinical clarity and empathetic service delivery.

### Visual Philosophy & Style
The design model follows **Structured Institutional Clarity**—a modern enterprise synthesis combining high-utility data density, clinical legibility, and calm, distraction-free surfaces. It rejects decorative trends like glossy neomorphism or vaporwave in favor of crisp structural containers, intentional status signposting, and low eye-strain palettes optimized for prolonged desktop reviews and field tablet data entry under intense lighting conditions.

### Emotional Target & Experience
- **Authoritative & Trustworthy:** Institutional indigo structural chrome conveys statutory governance, compliance, and procedural rigor.
- **Urgent Yet Composed:** Clinical teal and emerald communicate life-affirming care and operational health, while high-contrast triage tokens (amber, rose) isolate epidemiological outbreaks without causing cognitive panic.
- **Accessible & Resilient:** Tailored for multilingual public service workflows (English, Hindi, and local scripts) with unambiguous data hierarchy, robust hit targets, and distinct visual validation states.

## Colors

The palette organizes systemic functions into authoritative chrome, vital clinical actions, multi-tier epidemiological alert levels, and neutral baseline structures.

### Functional Roles & Allocation

- **Structural Chrome & Navigation:**
  - Deep Navy Slate (`#0F172A`, `#1E293B`): Fixed side navigation bars, high-level headers, and global system shells.
  - Deep Midnight Indigo (`#1E1B4B`): Dedicated contextual filters and secure role switchers.
- **Primary Clinical Action & Health Health Indicators:**
  - Clinical Teal 600 (`#0D9488`): Primary actions, verified patient cohort CTA, and focused form outlines.
  - Clinical Teal 500 (`#14B8A6`): Interactive hover states and active navigation indicators.
  - Health Emerald (`#059669`): Successful immunizations, positive metric trends, and verified field logs.
- **Epidemiological Surveillance Severity:**
  - High Alert Rose (`#E11D48`): Epidemic triggers, maternal emergency alerts, acute malnutrition flags, and destructive operations.
  - Alert Crimson (`#BE123C`): Critical threshold breaches and dark-mode high-contrast warning badges.
  - Cautionary Amber (`#F59E0B`): Delayed supply runs, unverified survey batches, and pending field validations.
  - Informational Sky (`#0284C7`): Training updates, passive district bulletins, and geographic sync indicators.
- **Canvas & Surface Neutrality:**
  - Base Canvas (`#F8FAFC`): Background canvas minimizing glare.
  - Surface Tier 1 (`#F1F5F9`): Inset tables, rail backgrounds, and disabled field surfaces.
  - Surface Raised (`#FFFFFF`): Primary cards, floating modal dialogues, and tabular datasets.
  - Border Subdued (`#E2E8F0`): Structural card definitions and row delineators.
  - Text Primary (`#0F172A`), Text Secondary (`#475569`), Text Muted (`#94A3B8`).

## Typography

The typographical structure is anchored in **Inter** for its clarity across micro-data displays and full Unicode support across Devanagari character sets (Hindi/Chhattisgarhi localization).

### Typography Guidance & Multilingual Considerations
- **Tabular Figures (`tnum`):** All quantitative displays—including case counts, immunisation percentages, block mortality, and timestamps—must mandate CSS `font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums` to ensure strict vertical alignment in tables and diagnostic dashboards.
- **Bilingual Stacking:** When labels feature Hindi accompanied by English translations, the Hindi string is positioned first with a weight adjustment (`FontWeight: 500`), while the auxiliary English secondary string sits below using `body-sm` (`#64748B`).
- **Hierarchy Enforcement:** Section overviews require crisp distinction between operational metrics (`metric-xl`, `metric-md`) and informational metadata. Uppercase tracking is reserved exclusively for system micro-labels (`label-sm`).

## Layout & Spacing

The layout model enforces a strictly structured, high-density 12-column grid system tuned for surveillance command hubs, while transitioning to single-column responsive views on handheld field devices.

### Grid & Viewport Mechanics
- **Desktop (1280px+):** 12-column responsive grid with a collapsed 64px icon rail or expanded 260px administrative navigation sidebar. Margins sit at `2rem` (`32px`) and gutters at `1.25rem` (`20px`). Content max-width is unbound for wide operational surveillance displays, capping dashboard panels at `1600px` for optimal ocular span.
- **Tablet / Large Mobile (768px – 1023px):** 8-column layout with standard `1.5rem` (`24px`) margins and `1rem` (`16px`) gutters. Side rails fold into contextual off-canvas navigation sheets.
- **Mobile Handheld (320px – 767px):** 4-column layout with `1rem` (`16px`) margins and `0.75rem` (`12px`) gutters. Data grids collapse into vertical block-card lists with expanded touch heights (min 44px).

### Spacing Rhythm
Spacing adheres to a base 4px/8px modular cadence. Internal card paddings use `space-md` (`16px`) for standard cards and `space-lg` (`24px`) for high-level summaries. Compact rows and inline triage badges scale with `space-xs` and `space-sm`.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses **tonal layer differentiation paired with structured outlines**, minimizing fuzzy skeuomorphic shadows in favor of precise contrast boundaries suited to high-glare environments.

### Surface Tiers & Stacking
- **Canvas Base (`#F8FAFC`):** Lowest layer; hosts background layout structures and section separators.
- **Surface Inset (`#F1F5F9`):** Recessed containers, including table filter panels, progress rails, and deactivated controls.
- **Surface Default (`#FFFFFF`):** High-density cards and operational dashboards. Defined via structural border: `1px solid #E2E8F0` with a subtle elevation anchor: `box-shadow: 0 1px 3px 0 rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.05), 0 1px 2px -1px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.05)`.
- **Surface Raised / Overlays (`#FFFFFF`):** Dropdowns, quick-filters, date-pickers, and hover cards: `box-shadow: 0 4px 6px -1px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.08), 0 2px 4px -2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.06)`, framed with a crisp boundary `1px solid #CBD5E1`.
- **Surface Modal / Alert Floats:** Command dialogues, emergency dispatch overrides, and active floating assistant modules: `box-shadow: 0 20px 25px -5px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.12), 0 8px 10px -6px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.08)`, bounded by `1px solid #94A3B8`.

## Shapes

The design system establishes a calculated balance between sterile utility and approachable health-worker software, utilizing precise corner treatments across functional modules.

### Shape Language & Geometry
- **Primary Content Blocks & Cards:** Set precisely to `rounded-lg` (`0.5rem` / `8px` to `12px`), delivering clean visual containment without clinical harshness.
- **Status Tags, Severity Chips, & Pills:** Standard badges implement `rounded-md` (`6px`) to reinforce tabular structure, while floating AI assistants, quick-filter chips, and field counters adopt a full pill shape (`rounded-full` / `9999px`) to immediately signal interactive agility.
- **Form Controls & Inputs:** Form fields and dropdown triggers feature uniform `8px` (`0.5rem`) corner rounding, matching primary button geometry for structural alignment.

## Components

### Buttons & Interactive Controls
- **Primary Action (Clinical Teal):** Background `#0D9488`, white label, 8px border radius, 40px standard height (48px on touch). Hover: `#0F766E`. Active: `#115E59`. Focus: 2px offset with ring `#14B8A6`.
- **Destructive / High Alert Action:** Background `#E11D48`, white label, hover `#BE123C`. Used for emergency triage escalation, maternal risk referrals, and severe containment flagging.
- **Secondary / Operational Outline:** Background transparent, 1px border `#CBD5E1`, text `#1E293B`. Hover: `#F1F5F9`.

### Chips, Badges & Surveillance Severity Tags
- **High Alert (Severe Risk / Epidemic):** Background `#FFE4E6`, border `1px solid #FDA4AF`, text `#9F1239`. Includes high-visibility pulsing dot (`#E11D48`) for live cluster anomalies.
- **Warning / Review Required:** Background `#FEF3C7`, border `1px solid #FCD34D`, text `#92400E`.
- **Positive / Verified Field Data:** Background `#ECFDF5`, border `1px solid #A7F3D0`, text `#065F46`.
- **Informational / General Sync:** Background `#F0F9FF`, border `1px solid #BAE6FD`, text `#0369A1`.

### Data Tables & Operational Grids
- **Header Structure:** Height 44px, background `#F8FAFC`, uppercase `label-md` `#475569`, border bottom `1px solid #E2E8F0`.
- **Data Rows:** Height 48px standard (56px for bilingual row entries). Default background `#FFFFFF`, alternative/hover background `#F8FAFC`. Pinned left columns for worker name/ID.
- **Cell Content:** Tabular numbers with right-aligned metrics; left-aligned identifiers and contact entities.

### Forms & Input Fields
- Height 40px (desktop) / 48px (tablet/mobile). Base background `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid #CBD5E1`, text `#0F172A`. Placeholder `#94A3B8`.
- Focus ring: `2px solid #0D9488` with `0px` gap for precise keyboard navigation. Error border: `1px solid #E11D48`.

### Surveillance Cards & KPI Modules
- 12px corner rounding, `1px solid #E2E8F0` border, white surface. 
- Layout: Top-row label with severity dot indicator; prominent `metric-xl` figure; bottom trend line pairing tabular variation percentages with contextual baseline windows (e.g., `+12.4% vs last week`).

### Floating Assistant (Sahayak AI Window / Pill)
- **Resting Pill Token:** Positioned at bottom-right (`24px` inset), height 48px, background `#0F172A`, text `#FFFFFF`, icon accent `#14B8A6`, full pill shape (`9999px`), shadow tier modal.
- **Expanded Panel:** Dimensions 380px wide × 560px max height, radius 16px, background `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid #CBD5E1`. Equipped with speech-to-text audio waveforms, Hindi translation toggles, and direct field referral generators.
